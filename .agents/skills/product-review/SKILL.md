# Product Review

Review a PR or plan for surface area completeness — ensuring that every place a feature appears in the product is
accounted for, that multi-user collaboration scenarios are considered, and that AI agent interaction points are covered.

Read [review-process.md](review-process.md) for the full review workflow and checklist.
